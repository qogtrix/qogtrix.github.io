3D Modelling Workspace STAGE 5

3D Modelling Workspace

Real parametric modelling: add geometry, edit its dimensions, cut holes, combine parts with real CSG booleans, assemble components, assign Stage 4 materials, and save the model into your project. All dimensions are stored in millimetres; switch the display unit any time.

Kernel: mesh-based CSG (three-bvh-csg), not a B-rep CAD kernel. Fillets and chamfers work on boxes only, gear teeth are a simplified profile, and STEP / 3MF / FCStd export is not supported — STL, OBJ, GLB and JSON are. See docs/MODELLING.md for the exact limits.
