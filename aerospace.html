Aerospace Engineering Laboratory

STAGE 10
Aerospace safety. Everything here is a conceptual / preliminary design analysed by real (simplified) engineering models — thin-airfoil theory, lifting-line corrections, Tsiolkovsky's equation, Barrowman stability, a 2-D point-mass trajectory. Nothing on this page is flight-certified engineering. XFOIL, AVL and OpenVSP are not installed here, so there is no panel-method or CFD analysis. A completed calculation never makes an aircraft, rocket or drone safe to fly. Real flight hardware needs validated tools, wind-tunnel or flight testing, and qualified review.
