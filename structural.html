Structural Engineering Laboratory

STAGE 9
Structural safety. Everything here is a conceptual / preliminary design analysed by a real direct-stiffness solver. Nothing on this page is validated for construction. A completed analysis is not a code check and never makes a structure safe to build. Real structures need a licensed structural engineer, local codes (IS 800, IS 456, IS 875, IS 1893, AISC, Eurocodes), connection design, foundations, and site-specific loads.

    The solver itself is a first-order, linear-elastic direct-stiffness analysis: no P-delta / second-order effects, no system buckling (member Euler/Johnson only), no plate/shell/solid finite elements (CalculiX, Code_Aster and Elmer were checked — none is installed on this machine), no connection design, no foundation design. Treat every result as a preliminary, educational check.
