Robotics Engineering Laboratory

STAGE 10
Robotics safety. Everything here is a conceptual / preliminary design analysed by real kinematics, control and collision models. Nothing on this page is validated for a real robot or a safety-critical application. Joint torques are checked against a steady-state DC motor model and link stresses against the Stage 9 beam solver — there is no full dynamics simulation, no safety-rated controller, and no real-world testing. PyBullet / ROS / MoveIt are not installed here.
