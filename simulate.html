Simulation Workspace STAGE 6

Simulation Workspace

Two honest kinds of simulation live here. Rigid-body dynamics runs the real Rapier engine in your browser (validated against analytical benchmarks — see docs/VALIDATION_STAGE6.md). Analytical solvers run closed-form engineering formulas on the backend and are labelled as analytical — they are not finite-element results. FEA and CFD are not installed in this environment, so the job system records those jobs as not implemented instead of faking results.

Choose a project and load its model.

Physics

Fixed timestep 1/60 s. Bodies are the model's top-level components; children move rigidly with their parent. Collision shapes are bounding boxes (a ball for spheres) — an approximation, stated in the results. Mass comes from the component's mesh volume × its Stage 4 material density (1000 kg/m³ when no material is assigned).
