Mechanical Engineering Laboratory STAGE 7

Mechanical Engineering Laboratory

Safety note: everything here is a conceptual design. Calculations are analytical formulas and the geometry is simplified (for example, gear teeth are a trapezoidal approximation, not a true involute). Nothing on this page is validated for manufacturing or real-world operation. Do not build a machine from it without a qualified engineer's review.

Design mechanical assemblies from the component library, run gear-train and shaft calculations, animate a crank-slider, and generate a bill of materials — all on real model data and the Stage 4 material database.

Mechanism

Exact slider-crank kinematics: x(θ) = r·cosθ + √(l² − r²sin²θ). The animation is the formula, not a physics guess.

Mechanical calculations are analytical formulas (see each solver's assumptions). They are labelled analytical — not FEA. A missing material property stops the run with a named error; nothing is guessed.
