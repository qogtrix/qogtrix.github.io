Electrical safety. Everything here is a conceptual design analysed by a real circuit solver. Nothing on this page is validated for a real electrical installation. Voltages above 50 V are flagged as hazardous; never touch a live circuit, and never build mains-voltage hardware from an unreviewed design. If a component's power exceeds its rating, the result says so — believe it.
